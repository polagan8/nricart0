A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before the
first spoonful.

How do I choose my pickle?+

Start with the flavours you enjoy: mango for a tangy bite, gongura for a leafy sour note, or chicken and prawn for a savoury option. Open a product to explore its flavour profile.

Can I order from outside India?+

Availability depends on the product and destination. Contact NRICart before ordering to confirm delivery options, costs and any restrictions for your country.

Where can I find ingredients and storage advice?+

Use the verified label on your jar for ingredients, allergens, shelf life and storage. The preview descriptions here are not a substitute for the product label. Ask NRICart for the label before ordering.

Need help with an order?+

Reach NRICart on WhatsApp at +91 94946 08143. Send your order reference if you already have one.
